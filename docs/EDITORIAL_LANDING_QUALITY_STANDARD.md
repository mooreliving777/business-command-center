# Editorial Landing Page Quality Standard
Status: **draft for human review** — 2026-11-09
Scope: Public-facing marketing pages for the Moore business network and future Moorvio pages. Each brand keeps its own voice, assets, color palette, typography, and visitors' next action. This complements `docs/LANDING_PAGE_STANDARD.md` and does not authorize publishing.

## Five anti-template checks
1. **Hero:** aim for 6–10 words (hard maximum 14) in the primary H1. A visitor should immediately know the offer or purpose. Keep one H1 per page.
2. **Typography:** do not blindly reuse Geist, Fraunces, or Inter. Those typefaces are not prohibited, but require a demonstrated reason; choose a distinct readable pairing per brand. Audit theme-wide fonts as well as inline styles.
3. **Highlight discipline:** do not color random words in an H1 solely for an AI-generated aesthetic. Color establishes brand hierarchy, navigational affordances, or necessary status.
4. **No decorative pill/dot clutter:** avoid repeated rounded badges, glowing dots, generic status pills, and endlessly boxed feature cards. A badge is justified only by a real status or meaningful category.
5. **Remove meaningless eyebrows:** no "THE FUTURE OF" or numbered section taglines above every heading. Use editorial headings with factual support and visible whitespace instead.

## Experience requirements
- Mobile-first at 320px, 375px, 768px and desktop; headings reflow without clipped words or cropped faces.
- Visible focus states, semantic heading levels, contrast-checked text, labels for forms, reduced motion, performant assets.
- Use authentic licensed photography or existing brand-approved media. Do not invent creator likenesses, merchandise photos, testimonials, customer counts, awards, prices or claims.
- Every CTA points to a working, verified destination; service pages preserve existing contact forms and booking paths.
- Prefer varied editorial compositions: split hero, full-bleed photography, text-led essays, visual case studies and open feature rows. No cookie-cutter card stack repeated across every brand.
- A site's existing strong short H1 should be retained; quality improvement is not a mandate to rewrite good copy.

## Business-specific acceptance
- **Moorvio:** CRM-first, feature-status truth, no fictitious signups, pricing, integration or testimonials. Design Studio is later.
- **City Web Design Pros:** $99 WordPress Rescue eligibility and exclusions remain clear; associated-business sites are labeled accurately, not passed off as external client results.
- **Creator/education pages:** offer immediate practical value and a clear free next step; avoid automatic-income claims or forced signups.
- **Entertainment and talent:** authentic media credits and rights, professional partnership contact paths, no unsupported audience statistics.
- **The Harsh Truth:** concepts vs real inventory visibly distinguished; requests are nonbinding, approval is manual, no fabricated variants/shipping/prices, no forced checkout.
- **Service and community brands:** never imply programs, aid, bookings or openings are available before verification.

## Review / publication gates
1. Preserve original live page, links, forms, navigation and metadata. Build in draft or unpublished theme first.
2. Compare before/after screenshots on mobile and desktop. Test forms, links, consent, keyboard, contrast and SEO.
3. Read actual page facts and inspect stock/rights/fees. No placeholders or invented social proof.
4. Human approval is mandatory before theme activation, live page replacement, deployment, product checkout, new subscriptions, or any spending.
5. Record draft URLs and the edit/review outcome. Do not merge this PR or publish merely because the automated checks pass.
