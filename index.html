09 / FAQ

A few clear answers.

Can I sign up and use Moorvio today?

Not through this site. This is a public development showcase, not an authenticated product or a public waitlist.

What is being developed first?

A dependable customer relationship management workflow, including leads, follow-ups and related projects. Design Studio comes later.

Does the AI act without approval?

The intended workflow is review-first. Hosted AI spending and autonomous publishing are not enabled as part of this showcase.

Are online payments and payroll live?

No. Existing application summaries discuss manually recorded invoice payments and payroll preparation only. This site neither collects payments nor processes payroll.

How can I follow the project?

Visit the public GitHub repository for the project overview and code changes. There is no verified email signup on this page.
